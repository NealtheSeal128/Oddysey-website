The story

A voyage home

After the fall of Troy, the soldier Odysseus turns for Ithaca — and spends ten years finding his way back. He blinds the Cyclops, slips past the sirens, and loses his crew one by one, while at home his wife Penelope holds off suitors and his son Telemachus grows into a man. Our student adaptation condenses that journey into a single evening of theatre.
